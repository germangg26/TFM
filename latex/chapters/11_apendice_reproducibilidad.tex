\chapter{Reproducibility}
\label{ap:reproducibilidad}

This appendix gathers the information needed to reproduce the results of this work: the execution environment, library versions, random seeds, the notebook execution order, the organisation of the data and the validation scheme. The aim is for a third party to be able to regenerate the full \textit{pipeline} and obtain the same figures. The full source code (without the campaign data, which is private) is available in the repository \url{https://github.com/germangg26/TFM}.

\section{Random seeds}

All processes with a random component fix the seed \textbf{42} to guarantee reproducibility: data sampling (\texttt{random\_state=42}), the initialisation of \textit{numpy} (\texttt{np.random.seed(42)}), the cross-validation splits, the \textit{bootstrap} (\texttt{RandomState(42)}, 1000 repetitions) and the models (\texttt{random\_state=42} in LightGBM, \textit{scikit-learn} and the autoencoder). In the autoencoder (M0), TensorFlow operation determinism is additionally enabled (\texttt{TF\_ENABLE\_ONEDNN\_OPTS=0} and \textit{enable\_op\_determinism}) so that the embedding is identical across runs.

\section{Data}

The raw campaign data (\texttt{data/raw/}) are not versioned because of their size and private nature. From the $\approx 2$ million original events (2.026.166) a \textbf{balanced 1:1 sample} is taken (all clicks plus an equal number of non-clicks), which yields a training prevalence of $\approx 0{,}238$; the true production prior ($\approx 2$\,\%) is restored through the \textit{prior} correction (see Chapter~\ref{cap:propension}). The detailed demographic and labour variables (labour status, marital status, household, vehicle, etc.) are \textbf{synthetic}: they are generated by sampling from official distributions (INE, municipal censuses, DGT) anchored to the user's postal code, with the limitations this entails for individual inference (see Chapter~\ref{cap:datos}).

\section{Validation scheme}

To avoid information leakage, three schemes are used depending on the case: \textbf{GroupKFold grouped by user} (5 \textit{folds}) in M2, so that no user appears simultaneously in training and validation; \textbf{temporal cross-validation} (expanding-window \textit{TimeSeriesSplit}) in the \textit{warm} model and in the \textit{forecast}, respecting the chronological order; and \textbf{reconstruction of the segment using \textit{train} only} in the evaluation of the recommender (M3). Uncertainty is quantified with \textbf{\textit{bootstrap}} (1000 repetitions, seed 42) and 95\,\% confidence intervals, and multiple comparisons are corrected with the Holm method.
